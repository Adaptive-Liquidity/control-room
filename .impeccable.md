## Design Context

### Users
Marketing operators at Adaptive Liquidity / AEON Protocol — editors, managers, and admins who live in this tool daily to create, review, schedule, and attribute content. Context is focused work sessions, not browsing. Primary jobs: clear the approval queue, draft compliant content, monitor agent status, and understand what content drives signups/integrations.

### Brand Personality
Precise · Institutional · Calm

Emotional goals: confidence and clarity under regulatory pressure. The UI should feel like a control room for serious operators — architectural, evidence-backed, never hype.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme:** Light mode institutional command center
- **References:** Linear, Stripe Dashboard, quiet Bloomberg terminal density — scannable, restrained, professional
- **Anti-references:** Neon teal cyberpunk dashboards, AI-slop dark mode with glow/gradients, emoji navigation, oversized `font-black` metric cards, purple gradients, cream+terracotta clusters, glassmorphism, “All Systems Operational” status theater
- **Typography:** IBM Plex Sans + IBM Plex Mono (not Inter)
- **Accent:** Deep ink blue, used sparingly (~10%); neutrals do the work
- **Motion:** Minimal functional feedback only; no pulse glows or decorative animation

### Design Principles
1. **Quiet confidence** — Hierarchy via weight, size, and space; color is an accent, not a vibe
2. **Ops density** — Comfortable information density without oversized chrome or hero metrics
3. **No AI cosmetics** — No neon, glow, emoji icons, gradient CTAs, or fake telemetry badges
4. **Compliance-first calm** — Surfaces that support careful reading and Guardian review, not spectacle
5. **One primary action** — Clear CTAs; secondary actions stay visually quiet
